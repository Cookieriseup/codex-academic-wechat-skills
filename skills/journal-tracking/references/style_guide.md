# Journal Tracking Style Guide

## Overall Shape

Use a phone-friendly WeChat layout inspired by the `Research Policy: Vol 52, Issue 10, 2023` example:

1. Header: `期刊追踪丨标题`.
2. Podcast placeholder: `播客丨`.
3. Welcome sentence: `欢迎大家来到“学术播客丨期刊追踪”栏目。`
4. Opening note: one paragraph explaining this issue/batch and why it is worth tracking.
5. `期刊概况`: journal ranking, field, publisher/society, issue or online-first metadata.
6. `本期速览`: compact numbered list of all papers, followed by a `期刊介绍` block at the end of this section.
7. `主题分组`: 3-5 groups when the issue has enough papers.
8. Numbered paper cards.
9. `原文摘录与剖析`: each paper/material should include at least 2 short original passages with analysis.
10. `本期值得优先读`: 3-5 recommendations with reasons.
11. `思考讨论`: topic-level open question, not the user's personal research.
12. Evidence boundary and manual-check notes.
13. QR image and right-aligned bold signature block.

## Journal Introduction Placement

Place `期刊介绍` at the end of `01 本期速览`, after the compact paper list. This block should be reader-facing rather than database-like: explain the journal's academic positioning, major fields, ranking/status, and why this issue or online-first batch deserves attention. Keep unverifiable rankings as `待人工核验`.

## Article Card

Each paper card should use this sequence:

- number;
- English title;
- Chinese title;
- authors;
- citation/source;
- DOI/URL;
- Abstract;
- 摘要;
- 关键词;
- 原文摘录与剖析: at least 2 short original passages, each followed by Chinese analysis;
- 阅读价值;
- 适合谁读.

If information is missing, leave it as `待人工核验` rather than guessing.

For every paper/material, select excerpts that show a striking claim, conceptual hinge, methodological move, key finding, or limitation. If only a title is available, mark `无法满足原文引用数量：仅题名可见`. If only an abstract is available, quote from the abstract and label the evidence boundary.

## Tone

Use the "刷手机速览" advantage of the case materials without copying their wording:

- concise but not shallow;
- enough English bibliographic precision for academic readers;
- Chinese explanation should be natural, not translation-heavy;
- summaries should help readers compare papers, not merely restate abstracts.
