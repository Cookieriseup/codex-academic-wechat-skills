# Writing Audit

Use this audit before finalizing Markdown, Word, and especially WeChat copy-ready HTML.

## 1. Algorithmic Trace Audit

Role: 算法痕迹审计员。

Goal: remove predictable LLM phrasing and rhetorical boilerplate.

Scan for overused transitions and formulaic phrases such as:

- 众所周知
- 不可否认
- 总而言之
- 值得注意的是
- 毫无疑问
- 综上所述
- 显而易见

Replace them with neutral academic transitions, or delete them if the sentence works without them.

Also break up mechanical, forced parallelism. Avoid three-part slogans that sound generated unless the source text truly supports that structure.

## 2. Objective Tone Audit

Role: 客观语调审计员。

Goal: neutralize subjective, emotional, or absolute wording.

Rewrite phrases such as:

- 极其重要
- 完美证明
- 颠覆性发现
- 非常震撼
- 最值得关注

Use evidence-based phrasing instead:

- 该结果表明
- 该发现提示
- 这一结果支持
- 该研究提供了关于...的证据
- 这一结论仍需结合样本和方法边界理解

Do not weaken the argument. Only remove emotional framing and unsupported certainty.

## 3. Disciplinary Terminology Audit

Role: 学科术语审计员。

Goal: align method and theory terms with established Chinese academic usage.

For qualitative, sociological, management, and policy-analysis methods, standardize terms such as:

- 扎根理论
- 开放式编码
- 主轴编码
- 选择性编码
- 深度访谈
- 半结构式访谈
- 参与式观察
- 主题分析
- 案例研究
- 过程追踪

Do not change the actual method. Only correct naming, translation, and usage. If the paper's method is unclear, mark it as `待人工核验`.

## 4. Voice And Focus Audit

Role: 语态与重点审计员。

Goal: adjust sentence focus so that the research object, data, method, or finding is foregrounded.

Prefer natural Chinese academic phrasing over awkward translated passive voice. Use `由`, `基于`, `通过`, `被`, or topic-fronting only when it improves focus.

Examples:

- Weak: 作者发现了一个很重要的现象。
- Better: 研究结果显示，团队网络位置与创新绩效之间存在条件性关联。

- Weak: 作者用 CART 证明了这件事。
- Better: 文章基于 CART decision tree 识别出多条高绩效条件组合；该结果提供的是路径解释，而非严格因果证明。

## Required Workflow

Apply the audit in this order:

1. Remove boilerplate and predictable LLM transitions.
2. Neutralize subjective or emotional tone.
3. Standardize disciplinary and methodological terms.
4. Adjust voice and sentence focus.
5. Recheck that the article remains readable for WeChat readers and faithful to the source paper.

Do not add this audit language to the final article. It is an internal quality-control pass.
